## 🌍 Why PralayDrishti?

During disasters, every second matters. Rescue teams often receive incomplete, unstructured, and scattered emergency information, making it difficult to identify who needs help first.

**PralayDrishti** bridges this gap by transforming raw civilian SOS reports into actionable intelligence, helping emergency response teams make faster, smarter, and more informed rescue decisions.

### 🚀 What Makes PralayDrishti Different?

- **AI-Powered SOS Intelligence:** Processes unstructured emergency messages and extracts meaningful information.
- **Multilingual Voice SOS:** Enables victims to report emergencies in Hindi, English, and Hinglish.
- **Time-to-Criticality (TTC):** Dynamically prioritizes victims based on the urgency of their survival situation.
- **Intelligent Rescue Prioritization:** Uses priority-based queue management to ensure critical cases receive attention first.
- **Real-Time Disaster Heatmaps:** Visualizes affected regions and emergency hotspots for better resource allocation.
- **Ultra-Low Bandwidth Reporting:** Supports emergency communication through lightweight micro-payloads, designed for unreliable network conditions.

### 🎯 Our Mission

To build a disaster-response ecosystem where technology doesn't just collect emergency information but converts it into life-saving decisions.

**Because in a disaster, saving time means saving lives.**

### 🔮 Vision

To create a scalable, accessible, and intelligent disaster management platform that empowers rescue forces, minimizes response delays, and ensures that no distress signal goes unnoticed.
